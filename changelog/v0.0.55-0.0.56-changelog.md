# v0.0.55 -> v0.0.56

Drop now has a public community area for Markdown donation stories and health
suggestions. Donation stories can include one image; uploads are validated,
auto-oriented, resized, converted to WebP, and stripped of embedded metadata.
Health suggestions remain text-only.

Published posts receive stable Unicode-friendly URLs, semantic article pages,
canonical and social metadata, `BlogPosting` structured data, and sitemap
entries. Draft, hidden, and deleted posts stay out of public results, member
drafts can be resumed after a reload, readers can report unsafe posts, member
exports and account deletion include community content, and operators can hide
or restore reported posts with a reason visible to the author.
